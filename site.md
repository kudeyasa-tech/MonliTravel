# MonliTravel — Site Plan

**Type:** Business / Company website
**Industry:** Travel Services
**Goal:** Attract new customers (lead generation)
**Design:** Minimalist & clean — generous whitespace, restrained palette, clear typography, subtle motion.

## Design Tokens
- **Colors:** Ivory background `#FAF9F6`, ink text `#1C1C1A`, muted gray `#7A7A72`, sage accent `#3E5C50`, sand highlight `#D9CDB8`.
- **Typography:** Headings in *Fraunces* (elegant serif), body in *Inter* (clean sans). Generous line-height, large type scale.
- **Layout:** Max width 1120px, section rhythm ~120px vertical padding, hairline dividers (`1px solid rgba(28,28,26,.1)`).
- **Motion:** Scroll-reveal fade/rise, smooth anchor scrolling. Nothing louder than that.

## Page Structure
1. **Header (sticky):** Wordmark "MonliTravel" + nav (Destinations, Services, About, Contact) + CTA button "Plan Your Trip".
2. **Hero:** Full-viewport, travel image with dark scrim, eyebrow "BESPOKE TRAVEL SERVICES", H1 "Travel, thoughtfully arranged.", subhead, two CTAs, trust stats strip.
3. **Destinations:** Section intro + filter chips (All / Europe / Asia / Africa / Americas) + 6 destination cards (image, name, region, short line, price from).
4. **Services:** Numbered list layout (01–04): Itinerary Design, Curated Guided Tours, Private Transfers, 24/7 Concierge — each with a short paragraph; right column has an image.
5. **About:** Split layout — portrait image + "Since 2016…" story, checklist of differentiators, signature-style tagline.
6. **Testimonials:** Three quiet quote cards with names/roles and star accents.
7. **CTA Banner:** Sand-toned band: "Ready to plan your next journey?" + button scrolling to contact.
8. **Contact:** Two columns — form (name, email, destination select, message, submit w/ success state) + contact details (email, phone, address, hours, socials).
9. **Footer:** Wordmark, quick links, legal line.

## Conversion Path
Every section funnels toward either the hero CTA or the contact form; sticky header CTA always visible.
